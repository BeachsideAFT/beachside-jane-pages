Keeping this working

Good to know. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, so every link always shows the latest version — there is no separate share or re-share step. After a page is edited and the change is committed, it usually takes well under a minute to go live; refresh if you catch it mid-update.
Where these live. Every page here is hosted on GitHub Pages in the beachside-jane-pages repository, at beachsideaft.github.io/beachside-jane-pages. This hub is just a friendlier front door.
Printing. Both desk pages have a Save a copy of this page button at the bottom that opens the print dialog — choose Save as PDF to keep a dated record of what was ticked. The checklists clear themselves on a schedule, so print before they roll over if the record matters.

Where the June plan and the live account disagree

The Phase 1 Setup Manual and Build Plan (10 June 2026) describe a Beachside that does not exist. Five gaps, found by testing on 31 August and confirmed with Kerry.

The manual is built on a service we do not offer. Its whole patient journey starts with a prospect self-booking a free 15-minute Initial Consultation Call — and Beachside does not do those. It was a proposal, not a description. Sections 1, 3 and 8 of that manual all hang off it, so read those three as an idea somebody had in June rather than as instructions.

It also conflicts with how the practice actually works: the front office matches every client to a clinician with the right expertise, and online booking is deliberately off. That decision stands.
  • The good news is that this makes Phase 1 much smaller. All nine intake forms are already scoped to "all clients who book their first visit" — which is right, and needs no consultation call to hang off. They simply need switching from Sent manually to automatic, and narrowing so child forms reach child services. The real first appointments are the four Initial Assessment services; those are what the forms attach to.
  • Automatic wait list notifications cannot fire while online booking is off, and the offer tells the client to phone rather than carrying a booking link. Section 7 as written cannot work — and given the matching rule, the manual version is not what you want anyway. The manual, phone-back version is the correct one for Beachside.
  • The missed-session policy cannot be charged. No late cancellation fees exist in the account, so Jane offers only "no charge" when cancelling. Section 4.4 writes the policy; nothing enforces it.
  • Reminder timings drifted from the plan. The manual says email at 48 hours and SMS at 2 hours. The account is running email at 3 days and SMS at 2 days. Not wrong — just worth deciding on purpose rather than by accident.

What still needs a decision

  • Late cancellation fees have never been set up in Jane. Until they are, every cancellation is recorded as no charge and the missed-session policy cannot be enforced. This is the biggest single gap found while testing.
  • Eight of the nine intake forms are still set to send by hand. Switching them to automatic is the largest time saving available to the front office — and it does not depend on anything else being built first.
  • The wait list is on but unwatched, with stale requests on it. It needs an owner and a morning slot.
  • Rooms are set up but unused — fifteen clinicians have shifts with no room attached, so the room board tells you nothing.
  • Three Lucet fields could not be found where we expected them, including the one that controls whether the TPI sends. Somebody setting up a Kaiser client needs to look and report back.
